Your own research assistant. It runs on your machine, lives in the chat apps you already use, and turns scattered reading into something you can actually search.

What it does

Six things it takes off your desk.

Lives where you already talk

Telegram, Discord, Slack, Feishu, WeChat, DingTalk, email — no new app to open. Wherever you already type, it answers.

Reads your library

Point it at your Obsidian vault. Papers, notes and clippings get indexed where they sit, without moving a file.

A map that grows

Every source you add links its topics, methods and authors to what came before. The more you read, the clearer the shape of the field gets.

Skills you can add

arXiv, OpenAlex, image generation, your own scripts. If something is missing, write a skill and install it.

Works while you sleep

Scheduled jobs and automations keep collecting what you told them to collect, and have it waiting.

Any model you like

Switch providers from settings — whichever model fits the job, or the one you already pay for.

Wired into

Fifteen chat platforms, plus an OpenAI-compatible API for everything else.
